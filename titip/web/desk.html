    Autopilot from anywhere

    For mobile data or another Wi-Fi. Scan the code once with the phone's camera. It opens the Autopilot page only, never your Outlook mail.

    The code does not expire and it carries the key. Whoever has it can read the Autopilot page and press its buttons, so do not share a picture of it. “Make a new code” cuts off every phone that used the old one.

    Good to know

    Only paired phones get an answer. Your Outlook mail is never stored outside this PC and never goes through another server. Mail you send from the phone leaves from Outlook here, exactly as if you had typed it at your desk.

    Autopilot from anywhere is the one exception to “no other server”: its page (Gmail only) passes through a relay, encrypted on this PC with a key the relay never gets.

    Stop

    Stopping closes the door for every phone until you start Titip again. Paired phones stay paired.
