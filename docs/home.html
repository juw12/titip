Opening Titip

Put Titip on your Home Screen

The icon opens your mail and Autopilot from any network. You do not scan again.

  1. Tap Share in the bar of your browser.
  2. Choose Add to Home Screen, then Add.
  3. Open Titip from the new icon.

The icon takes the key to your PC along. For that, the address of this page holds the key for the next five minutes: do not copy or share it. After that, or when you leave, the key is taken out of the address again.
